About me

(Sandra)

I am a UX/UI and service designer with more than eight years of experience in digital products for the public and private sectors. I studied Art and Business Design at Universidad San Ignacio de Loyola and specialized in user experience at CENTRUM PUCP.

I listen to the people who use a service, turn complex requirements into clear journeys and test them with real users before they reach development. I work in agile teams alongside analysts, developers and user departments. I led the design of Postula 2.0 and the UX/UI team for the procurement module of SEACE 4.0, and I also design brands, graphic pieces, video and photography.
