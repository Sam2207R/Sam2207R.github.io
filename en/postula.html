Postula 2.0: applying to the Public Teaching Career

I designed the application, evaluation and management experience for teachers at institutes and higher-education schools, together with the user department and the technology department of the Ministry of Education of Peru.

Role
UX/UI design lead
Client
Directorate of Technical-Productive and Higher Education Services, Ministry of Education of Peru
Period
May 2023 to May 2024
Tools
Figma, Design System, interactive prototypes
SummaryProcessDecisionsScreensResult
Summary

Digitizing entry into the Public Teaching Career

The challenge

Law No. 30512 regulates the careers of teachers at institutes and higher-education schools. Postula 2.0 had to bring the whole entry process into a single system: registering, uploading a résumé, choosing positions, making a statement and taking the evaluation.

My role

I led the platform design and coordinated the design team's work, with review meetings with the user department, the technology department and front-end development.

Result

Flows and prototypes validated with specialists and teachers from five regions. Due to a lack of budget, the platform was not fully developed. Projected impact: more than 150,000 teachers benefited, 40% less time to apply and an end to data-entry errors.

Design decisions

Every flow, adjusted with what teachers said

Registration and sign-in

A long registration with ID, email and code had to feel guided.

  • Manual entry of first and last names, and a visual instruction for finding the geographic location code on the national ID (DNI).
  • Tooltips for the email field and a clear message about how to generate the code and when it expires.
  • Resend rule: up to 3 resends before expiry.
  • Sign-in with email, password and reCAPTCHA, integrated with the PerúEduca portal's single sign-on.

Résumé and position selection

Long forms organized into expandable sections, with validation in view.

  • Dropdowns by type of training and tooltips with the equivalences between hours and credits.
  • Filters by specialty, location, economic activity and study program.
  • Warnings when the chosen position has requirements the applicant does not meet.

Sworn statement and completion

Document upload with clear states so applicants know whether the upload worked.

  • Progress bar, confirmations and an alert before submitting.
  • Final screen with a receipt and instructions.

Assessment

Multiple-choice questions with a timer, navigation by question and alerts for unanswered questions.

Results

Results by competency with an option to download them.

Complaints and claims

Flow prioritized with the user department and designed as wireframes to cover every stage of the application.

Model lesson

Model-lesson upload with confirmations, designed to give applicants more time.

Result

A validated design, with a close that did not depend on the team

What was achieved

  • Registration, sign-in and password-recovery flows validated with technical specialists and process owners.
  • Design System and palette defined from tests with teachers from five regions.
  • Administrator and supervisor prototypes reviewed with the user department.
  • Assessment, results, model-lesson and complaints modules designed and reviewed with the user department and development.

What did not happen

Due to a lack of budget, the platform was not fully developed. The design and validation work remains as a documented foundation for picking it back up.

What I took away

Leading the design, organizing five roles under a single Design System and defending decisions with test data, on a project with real time and budget constraints.
